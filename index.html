Prototype rules

How to play

  1. Select a unit, then tap a staging slot. Tap a draft unit again to remove it.
  2. Fill all six available staging slots, with no more than two new units of each type.
  3. Each player presses Ready. The round resolves only after both players are locked in.
  4. Damage resolves first. Defeated pieces are removed, then surviving healers restore adjacent allies.
  5. Friendly columns advance together. White wins contested empty squares on odd rounds; Black wins them on even rounds.
  6. Draft units enter the board for the following round wherever the starting tile is clear.
  7. A staging slot remains available when its entry piece is predicted to advance; it is blocked only when the entry will stay occupied.
  8. Enable Random for either player to automate that side every second; enable both to watch a complete simulation.
  9. A unit crossing the opposite edge scores a breakthrough. First to five wins.

Both staging rows and both players’ units are visible during hot-seat testing.
