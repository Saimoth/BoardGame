Prototype rules

How to play

  1. Select a unit, then tap a staging slot. Tap a draft unit again to remove it.
  2. Fill all six available staging slots, with no more than two new units of each type.
  3. Press Play turn. Draft units lock, then enter the board at the start of your next turn.
  4. Units use their ability, then advance one square. Opposing units block each other until one is defeated.
  5. A full board column blocks its staging slot until a space opens; blocked slots count as filled.
  6. A unit crossing the opposite edge scores a breakthrough. First to five wins.

Both staging rows and both players’ units are visible during hot-seat testing.
