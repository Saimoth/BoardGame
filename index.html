How to play

  1. Select a unit, then tap a staging slot. Tap a draft unit again to remove it.
  2. Fill all six available staging slots, with no more than two new units of each type.
  3. Each player presses Ready. The round resolves only after both players are locked in.
  4. Both armies use their abilities simultaneously, then attempt to advance one square. Opposing units block each other until one is defeated.
  5. Draft units enter the board for the following round wherever the starting tile is clear.
  6. A full board column blocks its staging slot until a space opens; blocked slots count as filled.
  7. Enable Random for either player to automate that side every second; enable both to watch a complete simulation.
  8. A unit crossing the opposite edge scores a breakthrough. First to five wins.
